# 05 — Domain Dictionary

Use 3 to 7 of these domains when creating ideas:

- AI / Multi-Agent Systems
- Music Creation
- Meditation & Daily Rituals
- Respiratory Health / COPD
- Skilled Trades / Union
- Portable Energy / Solar
- Local / Midwest / St. Louis
- Habit Formation
- Creator Monetization
- Marketplace
- Offline-First Systems
- Knowledge Management

Strong ideas usually come from combining several of these together.
