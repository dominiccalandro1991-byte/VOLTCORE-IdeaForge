# 04 — Kill Rules

Kill the idea if any of these are true:

- Overall score is under 50
- Novelty is under 30 and Usefulness is under 40
- It is just a feature of an app you already have
- It needs impossible science
- It is only content/media with no real product
- You cannot clearly name who the main user is
- It is basically the same as another idea you already have

When you kill an idea, always write a clear reason.
