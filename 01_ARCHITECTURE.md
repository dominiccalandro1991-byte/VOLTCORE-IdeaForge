# 01 — Architecture

## Pipeline (Follow This Order)

1. Capture the idea
2. Combine domains (3–7 domains)
3. Score the idea
4. Kill or keep the idea
5. Enrich strong ideas
6. Create handoff package
7. Send to App Engineer
8. Log what you learned

## Important Rules

- Never skip scoring
- Never send an idea forward without a handoff package
- Keep killed ideas (do not delete them)
- Keep this system separate from your main projects
